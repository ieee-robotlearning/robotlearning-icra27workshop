Call for Papers

The workshop includes an open call for short, non-archival papers on robot learning in the era of foundation models, with emphasis on standardization, benchmarking, and real-world evaluation of robotic foundation models, particularly for mobile robots in unstructured environments. Selected contributions will be presented in a poster session, and three papers will be invited for spotlight presentations.

Topics of interest include:

  • What constitutes a fair trial when the world cannot be reset?
  • How do we distinguish genuine generalization from a fortunate test distribution?
  • How can policies be evaluated when they cannot be centrally executed on every platform?
  • How many trials are sufficient, and what must be reported alongside the number?
  • Which behavioral, robustness, safety, and intervention metrics are measurable in the field?
  • Which metrics should accompany task-success rate?
  • How should evaluation account for embodiment, environmental variation, and deployment cost?
  • Reproducible real-world benchmarking and reporting protocols for robotic foundation models

Submission site: OpenReview (link TBA)

Submission policy: We welcome new, short, non-archival submissions. Previously published work may also be considered for poster presentation if it is clearly relevant to the workshop and the authors disclose its prior publication. The workshop will accept up to 20 contributions. Formatting and page-limit details: TBA.

Review process: Contributions are selected for technical relevance, clarity, diversity of topics and participants, and fit with the workshop's focus. Authors of submitted papers will be asked to review at least one paper, and an independent Area Chair will oversee the review process. Three accepted contributions will be invited for spotlight presentations and the remainder for posters.
