Call for Papers

The workshop includes an open call for short, non-archival papers on robot learning in the era of foundation models, with emphasis on standardization, benchmarking, and real-world evaluation. Selected contributions will be presented in a poster session, and three papers will be invited for spotlight presentations. Student presentations are part of the program.

Topics of interest include:

  • What is a trial when the world cannot be reset?
  • How do we distinguish genuine generalization from a fortunate test distribution?
  • What replaces centralized execution when policies cannot be shipped to every platform?
  • How many trials are enough, and what must be reported alongside the number?
  • Which behavioural, robustness, and safety metrics are measurable in the field?
  • What constitutes a fair real-world trial?
  • How should generalization be measured?
  • Which metrics should accompany success rate?
  • How should evaluation account for embodiment, environmental variation, safety, intervention, and deployment cost?
